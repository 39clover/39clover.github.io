i’m not in my body anymore.
just orbiting it,
banished to rafters
of some spinning room.

this room loses syntax before i can sleep.
its ceiling swells—
an unpoppable balloon
pushing me further from the floor.

the blinds contract.
each expansion of my lungs
inflates a heavy head.
a vacuum tauts skin over a grid,
the skeleton kinks and clamps,
tightly pinching.
my mouth caves inward,
swallowing itself.

i reach to anchor my own geometry,
but miss

bits of me cling to walls,
while the rest hits tile
and splatters

a giant
focused into a thimble
